Using JobPilot

JobPilot provides job listings, resume analysis and matching tools. Match scores and model-generated categories are estimates to help with discovery; they are not guarantees of suitability or hiring outcomes.

Information and resumes

Account details, profile information, uploaded resumes and applications are stored to provide the service. Resume text is analyzed to extract skills and calculate recommendations. Recruiters can discover a resume only after its owner enables recruiter discovery in their profile, and recruiters can access resumes attached to applications for jobs they manage.

Your choices and responsibilities

Keep account credentials private, provide accurate application information, and share only resumes you are comfortable sending to an employer. You can disable candidate discovery in your profile and withdraw eligible applications from the tracker. Contact the support address configured by the site administrator for privacy requests or questions.

This is a product information page, not legal advice. A production operator should publish terms and privacy language reviewed for the applicable jurisdiction.
