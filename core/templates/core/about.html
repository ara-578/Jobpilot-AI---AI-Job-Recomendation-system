Add skills and upload a resume. The app extracts readable text, skills, education and experience.

02

03

Apply, track progress or, as a recruiter, publish roles and review ranked candidate profiles.

Responsible matching

Match percentages are relative ranking estimates, not probabilities of being hired. Category confidence is a model confidence output when available. Human review remains essential.
